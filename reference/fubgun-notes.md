		---If you cant afford the FF/FF set than use 1 more regular block/multi jewel and use 1 immutable force for psuedo stun immune

-Crafting Guide

-Staff (ilvl 83, 2 methods)

-1st method (also expensive)
1) You'll need to recomb a +4/+2/20% block together. This is done by doing 1 prefix+1suffix with 1 prefix (so +4 phys with 20% block and +2 spells).
Once you have your 3 mod staff, craft any affix and you'll need to use boat fracture and hope it hits 20% block, anyother outcome doesn't work.
2) Once you have your +4/+2/fractured 20% block staff, you need to craft "prefixes cannot be changed" and use veiled chaos with the boat, chose the veiled prefix option and hope to unveil %spell dmg
(if you don't hit spell damage, take an attack modifier like %phys and craft "cannot roll caster modifiers" and annul, this gives a 50/50 to remove the crafted mod or the veiled mod).
3) Last step is just crafting "prefixes cannot be changed" and either augment speed or reforge speed (reforge is 1/11 to fill suffixes but also substantially cheaper).

-2nd method (budget)
1) buy a staff with 20% block fractured or fracture one yourself
2) Use the fossil combo: Serrated, metallic and shuddering until you hit +2 spells (have to annul down to 1 prefix if it added multiple prefixes).
3) If your staff has 2 suffixes, craft "cannot roll attack modifiers". If your staff just has the fractured block suffix, than craft "can have 3 crafted modifiers, cannot roll attack and spell damage).
4) Aug phys on the harvest bench, this is a 60/40 for +3 or higher (hitting +4 is rare, this is why this craft is the budget method).
5) Craft "prefix cannot be changed" and use veiled chaos with the boat, hit a prefix with the unveil and hope to unveil spell damage
(if you don't hit spell damage, take an attack modifier like %phys and craft "cannot roll caster modifiers" and annul, this gives a 50/50 to remove the crafted mod or the veiled mod).
6) Last step is just crafting "prefixes cannot be changed" and either augment speed or reforge speed (reforge is 1/11 to fill suffixes but also substantially cheaper).

-gloves crafting (ilvl 84) - Need fractured warlock gloves with inc damage against chilled enemies
The easiest way to get gloves with this mod (called an incursion or temple modifier). You buy gloves on a bad base with this mod and use recombs to transfer it over - There is a trick here, if the temple gloves don't have anymore suffixes and your warlock gloves have screaming essence of rage, than you can never lose your temple modifier, this is a very useful trick when transferring the mod after you fail your fracture as well.
Once you have it on your warlock gloves, just need to 1/4 fracture (allflame fracture is generally not worth because it adds a ton of intangibility and it's pretty free to transfer the mod over to another base if you miss).
1) Use the fossil combo: Hollow - Sanctified - Prismatic (you want to hit t2 or better resist with an abyssal socket).
Depending on prices, it should almost always be worth to annul/exalt the suffix for t2+ resist if you miss (if you annul the abyss socket, have to use another fossil combo).
2) Once suffixes are done, you want to use an eldritch ember to make exarch dominant. Now we need to get t1% es
Craft mana -> exalt slam -> remove crafted modifier -> Eldritch annul (Repeat until you hit T1 %es)
3) Now with %es, craft "suffixes cannot be changed" and veiled exalt with allflame, hope to win 1 50/50 out of 2
4) Now before unveiling, craft "phys damage reduction while focused" to block it and than unveil your gloves. Any convert here is a win because you can swap the damage conversion with a rancour at the harvest bench
5) Gloves are done, craft your 6th modifier from the bench

-Rarity helmet (ilvl 84) - Need to fracture the "60% increased rarity of items dropped by slain rare or unique enemies"
This is pretty similar to the gloves and you can use the same trick to transfer the mods (read the gloves for the same explanation).
1) Use dense fossils, along with exalts/annuls and reforge "more likely" on harvest bench to finish the prefix (you reforge more likely when you have all 3 ES stats, but all low tier).
This is a pretty long process, if you have double t1 prefix I would recommend to fill your suffixes and go for an exalt slam with allflame
2) Once your prefixes are done, your suffixes are extremely easy. Just make Eater dominant (eldritch ichor) and Exalt/eldritch annul until you hit T1 resist (can optionally block intelligence between each slam as well).
3) craft the last resist and you're done

-chest crafting
Need an ilvl 86 twilight regalia, theres 2 ways to do this.
One you can try recombing for triple prefix, this is the easy and simple way to make a chest but wont work for quality bases, as it's too expensive to lose them over and over in recombs
With recomb method, it's just as simple as mashing 2 prefix + 2 prefix together (so %es/hybrid es and flat es/hybrid es for example) It'll eventually work
-How to craft a quality chest
1) Once you have a i86 over quality base, you'll dense fossil spam until double t1 prefix (i would prefer flat es and %es)
2) Now use an eldritch ichor, Exalt/eldritch annul until at least t2 resis (swap it to fire with harvest, as that resist is always useful)
Ideally you'll also craft int -> exalt slam -> remove int -> elditch annul (this way you block a large weighting mod).
3) Use a fracturing orb for a 3/4 chance of fracturing (make sure the chest only has 4 mods total).
Ideally you want to fracture a energy shield mod here, the resist isn't a great outcome, but still works.
4) As long as you didn't lose the 3/4 fracture, you can finish the chest - Proceed to dense fossils spam, exalt/annul/reforge more likely until prefix is done - You can also allflame exalt slam here when you have 2x t1 prefix and need a third (just remember to fill suffixes before)
5) once you finish prefixes, you just have to finish suffixes with the same method before
Whenever you hit a t1 resist suffix, I would use allflame exalt or depending on prices, a foulborn exalt to get the 2nd suffix
If you hit the 2nd resist, the chest is finished
6) use eldritch currency to finish your implicits
